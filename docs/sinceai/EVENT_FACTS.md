# Since AI 2026: event facts (from sinceai.ai field guide, updated 4 Nov 2026; read 5 Nov)

## Timeline (Turku = EET, same as Vilnius)
- Thu 5 Nov 15:00: public-safe summaries of all 15 challenges open in sinceai.app. Agree on at least three.
- Fri 15:00: check-in at EduCity, every member in person (required to share any prize).
- Fri 15:00-17:30: team lead marks team READY (roster then frozen), then selects ONE challenge. Final, first come first served, places limited. Team formation closes 16:45.
- Fri 16:45 MLH intro · 17:00 opening · 17:30-18:30 Jason Mayes (Google Web AI) keynote + tech talk.
- Fri 18:50-19:30: company briefing in its EduCity room (hard end 19:30). Full challenge + files open in sinceai.app at 19:30, only for teams that chose it.
- Fri 20:30 MLH GitHub Copilot workshop · 21:00 Google AI Studio workshop (BioCity, Presidenttiauditorio, 258 seats).
- Build: BioCity + Joki, around the clock from Fri 19:30.
- Sat 09:00-12:00 and 14:30-18:00: company Q&A stands at Joki (each company present >= 2 h; plan questions). Sat 12:00 BearingPoint workshop.
- Sun 10:00: HARD submission deadline in sinceai.app (not Devpost).
- Sun 10:00-12:00: every builder does 20 pairwise comparisons. Top 5 from 5 DIFFERENT challenges = finalists.
- Sun 10:00-13:00: company evaluators pick their top 3. Announced 13:30.
- Sun 14:00-15:10: finals, 4 min pitch + up to 6 min jury Q&A each. Ends ~15:30.
- Calendar feed: https://sinceai.ai/hackathon-2026/guide/calendar/builders

## Prizes
- Each challenge: EUR 2,000 / 750 / 250. FORCIT (Drill and Blast Cycle Intelligence): EUR 3,000 / 1,500 / 500.
- Overall winner (jury at finals): EUR 5,000.
- Optional, same project, tick on submission: 8 MLH categories (Gemini API, ElevenLabs, MongoDB Atlas, Snowflake, Tiger Data, Vultr, Entire, Solana), Red Hat OpenShift AI award EUR 1,000 (must meaningfully build/serve/monitor the model on OpenShift AI; evidence link + >= 40-char explanation; criteria: OpenShift use 35, business value 30, reliability 25, docs 10), FIWARE award (non-cash).
- Gemini entries give the Gemini project NUMBER, never the key.

## Demo video (on every vote card)
- About 2 minutes, English narration, captions (noisy hall, watched without sound).
- Screen recording of the product working, not slides. Problem in 1-2 sentences, then user action → AI action → result, then value.
- Never invent numbers. Mock data/simulation is fine if labelled as such. Cut loading time.
